# Lunar Lifeline
**One Water Tank. Two Health Bars. Zero Free Choices.**

An interactive Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer (ages 7–12) built for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**. Players manage an Artemis lunar outpost at Shackleton Crater over 7 Sols, balancing limited water and power across life support, radiation shielding, and scientific research.

## Core Engineering Trade-Off
- **Dual-Use Water Lifeline:** Models real NASA ECLSS constraints by forcing players to split a single water reserve between **Electrolysis (Breathable Oxygen)** and **Roof Radiation Shielding Bladders**.
- **Consequence-Driven Gameplay:** Every decision carries an immediate or delayed resource cost—no choice is free.

## Verified NASA Open Data Sources
- **NASA DONKI (Space Weather Database):** Models real Solar Cycle 25 flare events, including the **November 7, 2026 M1.2 flare** (`2026-11-07T12:26:00-FLR-001`, GOES-P EXIS) and the **February 1, 2026 X8.1 superstorm**, plus a live DONKI API feed toggle.
- **Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO Diviner):** Models real Lunar South Pole thermal extremes (+50°C sunlit rim vs. -230°C permanently shadowed crater floor).
- **NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR):** Grounds plant growth stress experiments and lunar regolith EVA hazards in real spaceflight biology findings.
