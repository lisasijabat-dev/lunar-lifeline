STEP 2 OF 2: FLIGHT TRAINING

How to Survive & Gather Data

1. Making Choices (Your Main Action)

Each day (Sol), you must choose how to run the base so you can keep doing science. Every choice has a real trade-off—gaining science or power always costs something else!

2. Your Tool: The Water Lifeline Slider

You only have ONE water tank. Slide left to send water to the Air Maker (Electrolysis) so your crew can breathe. Slide right to pump water into the Roof Shield to block space radiation.

3. Dangers to Watch Out For

Space Rays (Solar Flares): Giant blasts from the Sun hit the Moon with radiation—keep your Roof Shield at 60% or higher during storms! Freezing Shadows & Low Air: If Power, Water, or any astronaut's health bar hits 0%, you must evacuate without finishing your research.
