Welcome Aboard!

You are an astronaut sent on a mission to research outer space at Shackleton Crater on the Moon. Please input your crew names and pick their avatars to start your mission!

Objective

Survive and maximize your research until the mission ends after 7 Sols (Moon days). Each Sol, you must choose how to run your lunar outpost and Greenhouse Dome (where your Botanist grows space plants for food, oxygen, and research) while collecting at least 50 Science Points for Earth.

Tools to Help You Survive

You will have ONE water tank. The water can be sent to certain machines and converted into other substances that help you survive. Slide left to send water to the Air Maker so your crew can breathe. Slide right to pump water into the Roof Shield to block dangerous space rays.

Dangers to Watch Out For
  • Solar Storms (Space Rays): Giant blasts from the Sun hit the Moon with radiation. Keep your Roof Shield at 60% or higher during solar flares so your crew does not get radiation sickness.
  • Freezing Shadows & Empty Tanks: Lunar shadows plunge to minus 230 degrees. If Power, Water, or any astronaut's health bar reaches 0%, you must evacuate early without finishing your research.

NASA Open Data & Design Guide

NASA DONKI API: Sols 3 and 6 use real 2026 Solar Flare events (Nov 7, 2026 M1.2 and Feb 1, 2026 X8.1) to drive radiation shielding trade-offs.

LRO Diviner & OSDR: Models minus 230°C crater shadows, abrasive lunar regolith dust damage, and greenhouse plant stress.

Replayable Mastery: Combines a 50-point mission completion threshold with a persistent High Score tracker to encourage strategic experimentation.
