Welcome Aboard!

You are an astronaut sent on a mission to research outer space at Shackleton Crater on the Moon. Please input your crew names and pick their avatars to start your mission!

Objective

Survive and maximize your research until the mission ends. This mission lasts 7 Sols (Moon days).

Each Sol, you must choose how to run your lunar outpost and Greenhouse Dome (where your Botanist grows space plants for food, oxygen, and research), while collecting at least 50 Science Points to bring back to Earth.

Tools to Help You Survive

You will have ONE water tank. The water can be sent to either the Air Maker to convert into breathable air (via electrolysis), or the Roof Shield to block dangerous space rays.

Slide left to send water to the Air Maker. Slide right to pump water into the Roof Shield.

Dangers to Watch Out For

Solar Storms (Space Rays): Giant blasts from the Sun hit the Moon with radiation. Keep your Roof Shield at 60% or higher during solar flares so your crew does not get radiation sickness.

Freezing Shadows & Empty Tanks: Lunar shadows plunge to -230 degrees. If Power, Water, or any astronaut's health bar reaches 0%, you must evacuate early without finishing your research.

NASA Open Data & Design Guide

NASA DONKI API: Sols 3 and 6 use real 2026 Solar Flare events (Nov 7, 2026 M1.2 and Feb 1, 2026 X8.1) to drive radiation shielding trade-offs.

LRO Diviner & OSDR: Models -230°C crater shadows, abrasive lunar regolith dust damage, and greenhouse plant stress.

Artemis Base Camp Design: Incorporates NASA's inflatable habitat dome, greenhouse bio-module, vertical polar solar arrays, and 80% post-landing reserve baseline.
